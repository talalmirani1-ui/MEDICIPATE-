Update 2026-08-22:
- NOMES now shows the existing NUMS subjects year-wise using the existing MBBS/NUMS year map.
- Subject options display the actual compatible NUMS MCQ count for the selected year + subject.
- Explicitly year-tagged NUMS questions are matched to that selected year.
- Legacy NUMS questions without a year are eligible only when their subject is explicitly offered in the selected NUMS year; they never fall back to another exam board or unrelated subject.
- No question records were duplicated or moved into a separate NOMES database.
